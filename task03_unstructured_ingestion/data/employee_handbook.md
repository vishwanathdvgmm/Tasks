# Employee Handbook

## Information Security

Employees must protect confidential company information. Store documents only in approved systems.
Never share credentials in email, chat, or source control. Report suspected security incidents promptly.

## Remote Work

Teams should document decisions in the project workspace. Keep meeting notes concise and identify
owners and deadlines for action items. Sensitive documents should use the approved access controls.

## Expense Policy

Employees must submit expense claims with receipts. A claim should include the transaction date,
business purpose, cost centre, and amount. Managers review claims before reimbursement.
